Task Board

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Task Board is a free task board that shows your own calendar events and task lists as a table, a board and a calendar. It runs entirely in your web browser. This page explains what it does with your data.

What the app accesses

Only after you sign in and agree, and only for your own account:

  • Google: your Google Calendar events (calendar.events), the list of calendars you see in Google Calendar (calendar.calendarlist.readonly, so events from shared and subscribed calendars show too) and Google Tasks (tasks).
  • Microsoft: your Outlook calendar (Calendars.ReadWrite), Microsoft To Do (Tasks.ReadWrite) and your basic profile (User.Read).

How it is used

The app reads your events and tasks to display them, and writes the changes you make (for example a new task, a status, a date or the order on the Priorities tab) back to your own Google or Microsoft account. It does nothing with your data other than what you ask it to do on screen.

Where your data is stored

Your events and tasks stay in your own Google or Microsoft account. Task Board has no server and no database: your browser talks directly to Google and Microsoft. The app's developer never receives, stores or sees your calendar, tasks or sign-in details.

Your browser keeps a few things on your device so the app works: a temporary sign-in token, your language, and display settings such as filters and saved views. Signing out removes the sign-in token.

Sharing

Your data is not sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Task Board's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Sign out in the app at any time. To revoke the app's access completely, remove "Task Board" at myaccount.google.com/permissions (Google) or myapplications.microsoft.com (Microsoft).

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the Google sign-in screen, or open an issue at github.com/berek-dev/task-board.
